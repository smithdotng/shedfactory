Most agencies talk about building products. We decided to actually run some.

Today Shedfactory designs, engineers and operates five platforms of its own: **[Diner](/products/diner)** for restaurants and venues, **[Glee](/products/glee)** for beauty bookings, **[Found](/products/found)** for property, **[Haulage](/products/haulage)** for moving goods across Nigeria, and **[74 Tribe Studios](/products/74-tribe)** for shows and studio hire.

Here's why we think that makes us a better partner for anyone with an idea.

## Launch day teaches things a brief never will

When a client hands you a specification, the hard questions have usually been answered — or quietly skipped. When the product is yours, nobody answers them for you. Who are the first hundred users? What happens when a payment fails at 11pm? Why did half the people who signed up never come back?

Running our own platforms means we've faced those questions ourselves. That experience shows up in every project we take on, often as the question nobody thought to ask yet.

## Two-sided platforms are hard. We've built five.

Every one of our products serves two groups at once: diners and venues, clients and stylists, buyers and realtors, shippers and operators, viewers and creators. Each side only joins if the other is already there.

That chicken-and-egg problem shapes everything — onboarding, pricing, what launches first and what waits. If your idea is a marketplace, a booking platform or a directory, it's a problem we know well.

## We feel the cost of our own decisions

Hosting bills, slow pages, confusing forms, a feature nobody uses — on our own products, we pay for those mistakes directly. That makes us careful, practical builders. We'd rather launch a focused first version that works than an ambitious one that doesn't.

## What this means if you're working with us

- **You get advice from operators, not just designers.** We'll tell you what to build first, and what can wait.
- **You get production-ready engineering.** The same standards that keep our platforms running go into yours.
- **You get a partner after launch.** Hosting, maintenance and growth are part of what we do every day.
